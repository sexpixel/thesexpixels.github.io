The Sex Pixels at 20

The Sex Pixels turns twenty: servicing clients since 2006. This new website is how we're marking it.

Twenty years of titles, promos, music videos, commercials and drama: BBC World Cup titles in 2010, Match of the Day's 50th anniversary titles, Nickelodeon, London Grammar, M.I.A. and Dimitri Vegas & Like Mike, and more recently on-set supervision on Gangs of London S4 and vendor work on Secret Service.

The studio has always paired traditional VFX craft with whatever new tools make filmmaking simpler. Today that means ethical AI and agentic coding alongside Nuke.

Here's to the next twenty.
