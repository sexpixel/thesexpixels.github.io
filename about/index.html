A filmmaker at heart, with twenty-six years in visual effects.

Today he still enjoys exploring methods and practices that use technology to simplify filmmaking. He is an agentic coder: every tool he builds is written with AI. He designs courses for the NFTS, ECAM Madrid and Escape Studios, and works through his boutique studio, The Sex Pixels.
