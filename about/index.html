Mark
Pinheiro

Filmmaker / VFX / AINuke compositor & VFX supervisorLondon

Filmmaker, Nuke compositor and VFX supervisor, with twenty-six years in visual effects.

His first composite, in 1995, was a VH1 promo that won a Promax Gold. Regular VFX work followed from 2000: on-set supervision, previz, compositing and post, across feature film, episodic, commercials and music promos.

He wrote the compositing course at Escape Studios in London, and still designs courses for the NFTS, ECAM Madrid and Escape Studios.

He holds an MA in Writing for Script & Screen from Falmouth University, and he is an agentic coder: every tool he builds is written with AI.

He works through his boutique studio, The Sex Pixels, pairing traditional VFX craft with ethical AI.
