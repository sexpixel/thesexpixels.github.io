Mark
Pinheiro

Filmmaker / VFX / AINuke compositor & VFX supervisorLondon

A filmmaker at heart, with twenty-six years in visual effects.

Mark's first composite won a Promax Gold in 1995, and he has worked steadily in VFX since 2000. The 2000s brought the Escape Studios compositing course. The 2010s brought stereo on Scorsese's Hugo, his first feature, Save The 365th Day, and a decade of award-winning shows. The 2020s brought a Masters in screenwriting, and finally accepting the role of department head.

Today he still enjoys exploring methods and practices that use technology to simplify filmmaking. He is an agentic coder: every tool he builds is written with AI. He still designs courses for the NFTS, ECAM Madrid and Escape Studios, and works through his boutique studio, The Sex Pixels.
