01The documentary · 2000–2014

In his own words

Eight showreels, each narrated by Mark: the stages of a career in VFX, told as a short documentary. It plays above, chapter by chapter; pick any chapter to jump to it, with sound.

A filmmaker at heart, with twenty-six years in visual effects.

Today he still enjoys exploring methods and practices that use technology to simplify filmmaking. He works through his boutique studio, The Sex Pixels. He is an agentic coder: every tool he builds is written with AI. He also designs courses for the NFTS, ECAM Madrid and Escape Studios.
